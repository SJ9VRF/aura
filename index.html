01 — Profile

A practice that connects model learning, agency, and deployment.

The work spans several layers that are often separated: learning and alignment at the model level, reasoning and action at the agent level, representation and planning at the world-model level, and the experimentation and engineering required to make these systems function outside a research prototype.

Model learning

Reinforcement learning, RLHF, self-play, distillation, representation learning, multimodal alignment, and post-training.

II

Agency

Computer use, long-horizon reasoning, autonomous UI navigation, tool interaction, multi-agent learning, and VLA systems.

III

World interaction

World models, 3D reasoning, robot learning, simulation, physical intelligence, evaluation, and sim-to-real.

08 — United AI Civilization
Founder · Public-Interest Initiative · YCIT-6

United AI Civilization

A global initiative examining what happens when increasingly capable AI systems move from tools to active participants in economic, institutional, and physical systems.
United AI Civilization connects frontier technical shifts — agents, multimodality, world models, reinforcement learning, and physical intelligence — with questions of human agency, institutional design, shared prosperity, and international coordination. The initiative develops a structured framework for reasoning from model capability to civilization-scale consequence, rather than treating AI governance as a layer separate from the technology itself.
The question is no longer only how to build better AI, but what institutions, norms, and coordination mechanisms are needed as intelligence becomes increasingly autonomous and embedded in the world.

09 — BrainButton™
Founder & CEO · The World's First Brain Fitness Club

BrainButton™

A daily brain-fitness system that translates neuroscience, cognition, and behavioral science into short, practical activations for focus, memory, creativity, and cognitive performance.
BrainButton™ is built around a simple product idea: people do not need more information about how the mind works; they need a repeatable way to act on the right insight at the right moment. The platform turns science into compact daily rituals, tracks the patterns that work for each person, and develops a personal library of cognitive tools that can be reused in real life.
One insight. One activation. One intentional step toward a stronger, more self-directed mind.
